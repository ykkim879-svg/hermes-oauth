hermes

A private, personal-use command-line tool for managing one's own Google account data (Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Sheets, Docs), and for personal financial record keeping.

Purpose

This application is used solely by its single developer/owner on their own machine, to read and update their own Google account data. It is not distributed, not offered as a service, and has no other users.

What it does

  • Reads and sends email on behalf of the owner
  • Reads and writes the owner's Google Sheets and Docs
  • Reads and writes the owner's Google Calendar events
  • Manages files in the owner's Google Drive
  • Reads the owner's Google Contacts
